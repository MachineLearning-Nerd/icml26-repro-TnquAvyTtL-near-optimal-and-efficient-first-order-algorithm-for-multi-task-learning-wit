# Limitations and deviations

- These are finite exact-RIP experiments satisfying a special case of the
  assumptions, not a formal proof of the universal high-probability theorems.
- The Claim 3 base uses \(d/T=32\) to isolate the leading \(dk/(NT)\) term.
  Hidden constants and suppressed logarithms are not recovered.
- Claim 5 validates a conservative sufficient margin on held-out seeds. It
  does not show that the expression is a necessary or tight phase transition.
- Empirical necessary-transition slopes for \(\kappa\) and \(\sigma_k\) are
  `-0.463` and `-1.006`, not the sufficient-condition exponents `+4` and `-2`.
  This mismatch is reported rather than hidden and is not used as a pass gate.
- The calibration branch failed its original gate and is preserved as a
  calibration-only historical route; it is not presented as accepted evidence.
