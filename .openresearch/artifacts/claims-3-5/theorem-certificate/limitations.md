# Limitations

The certificate checks the exact registered source statements and reconstructs
their displayed algebra. It is not a formalization of every appendix lemma and
does not recover hidden numerical constants. Finite TPGD results do not replace
the universal high-probability proof.

The full source archive is hash-pinned but is not duplicated in the text-only
candidate. The evaluator-visible candidate includes the exact relevant TeX
anchors and their hash.
